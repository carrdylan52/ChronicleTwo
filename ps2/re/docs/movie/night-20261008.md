## Result

`viBufRestartDMA__FP5ViBuf` stays guarded at **9/200** words. Two independent
blockers remain: the wrap branch's ring-count/mask register pair, and the tail's
`(volatile ViBuf *)` cast, which the draft needs to reload `env.d3madr`.

## Ring count and mask (allocator)

An LLDB capture of the 168-node GPR interference graph reproduces the canonical
9-word difference once the capture applies the Satan's Fiddle translation-unit
hooks: helper masks at `0x4324F0` (globals `0x53C990`/`0x53C994`) and the
direct-constant evaluate-first byte at `0x4A4AE3`. Without those hooks the plain
compiler gives 57 words here. Simulating simplify (`K = 25`) and colouring
reproduces every captured colour.

In the wrap branch the ring count `buf->n` is CSE temporary v43 (degree 25)
and the `0x0FFFFFFF` mask is v36. The PCode numbers user locals in reverse
declaration order (`fifo_bits` 62 … `index` 52), then split webs (`tag_addr` 51,
`mode` 50), then load CSE temporaries (wrap branch 43–47, else branch 37–41),
then the two mask constants (wrap 36, else 35). v36 is pushed first, so v43 sees
degree 24 when scanned, is pushed, and is coloured before the mask, taking `a2`.

Retail (`n` in `a3`, mask in `a2`) needs the mask coloured first: its number
must lie between v43 and v45 (`d4madr`). That alone leaves v43 at degree 25 when
scanned, which defers it to a later pass and gives `v0`. It also needs v43's
degree reduced by one, either by one fewer interference or by a low-degree
neighbour (`pos`, `mode`, a dma-count temporary) numbered below it. No single
renumbering of v43 and v36 across every slot reproduces retail.

Source probes this night:

| Probe | Restart words |
|---|---:|
| All 378 legal orders of the seven wrap-branch statements (size, qwc, tag, madr, mode, chcr, pos) | best 9 (4 orders), then 10, 12, 22… |
| Else-branch tag through `DmaAddr(buf->tag + index)` (SDK `TAG_ADDR` form) | 9 |
| Full SDK transcription (`bp`/`fp`/`ifc`, `IsInRegion` expanded, volatile registers) | 62, `0x318` body |

The SDK-form else-branch tag is neutral and is the cleaner spelling for any
future promotion.

Numbering evidence from captured variants:

- Reversing the branch order (`madr >= data` first) gives the first source
  branch the higher CSE and mask numbers: wrap `n` becomes v37 and its mask
  v35, the else branch's `n` v46 and mask v36 (103 words).
- Computing the wrap tag first gives `n` degree 24 but the same v43/v36
  numbering (12 words). `DmaAddr(buf->tag + 0)` and the SDK
  `else if ((index = …) != (fifo_index = …))` form keep v43/v36 (9 words).

Load CSE temporaries are therefore numbered per source branch, and both mask
constants come after all of them. Retail's order (mask above the wrap `n`) does
not follow from any statement or branch order tried; it needs a different
representation of the count or mask lifetime.

## Tail reload

Retail loads `env.d3madr` and `env.d3qwc` for the test and again for the
stores. Writing the stores as `*(volatile u32 *)… = buf->env.d3madr` (SDK
`D3_MADR` style), with plain pointers, or with only the first store volatile
gives 62 words: MWCC reuses the tested `d3madr` in place of reloading it.
The draft's `(volatile ViBuf *)` cast is not natural source, so a promotable
form needs another reason for the reload.

Retail's reload is `lw` with a `nop` in the branch delay slot, the shape of a
volatile load that the scheduler may not move. Named environment pointers do
not produce it:

| Tail form | Restart words |
|---|---:|
| t0: plain `buf->env` test and stores | 62 |
| t1: `sceIpuDmaEnv *env = &buf->env;` for the test and the stores | 64 |
| t2: the same pointer declared inside the store block only | 62 |
| t3: volatile register stores, plain `buf->env` reads | 62 |
