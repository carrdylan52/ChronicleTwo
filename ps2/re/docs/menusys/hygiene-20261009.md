# menusys hygiene pass (2026-10-09)

Scope: the functions matched on 2026-10-08/09 (`CalcTex__13CMenuItemInfoFv`,
`CheckEnableHaveItemNum__Fv`, `CommonSetMoveItemClass__FPA4_i`,
`LRCheck__13CMenuItemInfoFi`, `MenuDataSwap`, `MenuItemDebugDraw`,
`MenuPosFormValueSetCharaRobo`). Every change was checked with a PAL build
(`SCES_511.90: OK`) and `check_objects.py` (149/149).

## Changed
- `CMenuItemInfo::CalcTex`: the character arguments of
  `MenuItemCharaDataLoadEndCheckAfter` are `USER_CHARA_MAX/MONICA/ROBO`. The
  callee indexes `convtbl_4621[USER_CHARA_NUM][...]` (menuchr) and passes the
  number to `SetupUnitMan`, whose unit cases are Max/Monica/ridepod/monster.
- `CMenuItemInfo::LRCheck`: page 3 (ridepod) loads `USER_CHARA_ROBO`.
  `CheckLoadInfo(int chara)` treats 0/1 as the two characters' weapons and 2
  as `MenuUserParam.robo->parts`.
- `MenuItemDebugDraw`: both `Begin(6)` calls are `Begin(MG_PRIM_SPRITE)`; each
  is followed by two corner vertices and `mgCDrawPrim::Begin(int)` has one
  overload.

## Nothing to do
- `CheckEnableHaveItemNum`, `CommonSetMoveItemClass`, `MenuDataSwap`,
  `MenuPosFormValueSetCharaRobo`: no unnamed flags/modes with an owning enum,
  no no-op casts (every `(int)` converts a float; `(sceVif1Packet *) NULL`
  selects the `ReloadTexture` overload), no `unk_` accesses. Header blocks
  (`@mangled/@address/@size`) agree with `main.symbols.txt`.

## Left literal (no owning enum yet)
- `CMenuItemInfo::view_mode` pages: 0/1 character, 2 weapon, 3 ridepod,
  4 monster, 5 fishing rod (`view_form[6]` in CalcTex). About 115 uses in
  menusys; an enum belongs in one dedicated change.
- `key_arg_no` cursor layouts (`item_menu_argtbl[12]`).
- `MENU_ITEM_MOVE_INFO::from[0]` slot kinds: 0 character slot, 1 inventory
  board, 2 held item, 3 temporary (rewritten to 1), 4 overflow, 5 active bait
  (CommonSetMoveItemClass, MenuMoveItemPos, ExchangeItemInfoMake).
- Outside this scope: `CheckLoadInfo(2)` (6 sites) and `prim->Begin(6)`
  (9 sites) in other menusys functions take the same enumerators.
