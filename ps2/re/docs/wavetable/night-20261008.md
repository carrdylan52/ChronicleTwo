crossing a row's array bounds. Every horizontal neighbour uses typed
`now[row][column - 1]` / `now[row][column + 1]` indexing; all interior
row and column bounds remain 1 through 22. The seam also uses typed
plane/row indexing.

| New variant | Differing words / 324 | Body |

interior loop. All four existing native functions remain exact. Neither

so there is no admissible callee-scoped selector for its arithmetic

one statement at a time. In retail's unrolled interior, 1.9216 takes
`$f0`, 0.0196 `$f1` and 0.0015 `$f2`, although all three are
materialised at the row-loop head in source order (0.0196 first). The
draft colours them in materialisation order (0.0196 `$f0`, 1.9216 `$f1`).
