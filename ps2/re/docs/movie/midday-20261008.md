## Interrupt-control exclusion

Fresh `decompile.sh` output confirms `sync; ei` in `handler_endimage` and
both return paths of `vblankHandler`. The guarded `EIntr()` drafts still
measure 2/16 words (`0x38` versus `0x40`) and 42/56 words (`0xCC` versus
`0xE0`), respectively.

The current and `origin/master` SDK declarations provide an external
`EIntr(...)`; neither tree exposes a native `sync`/`ei` intrinsic or an
accepted use of one. A read-only scan of the pinned compiler's named
`__I_*` entry points finds no `__I_sync` or `__I_ei`. This scan is not proof
that every compiler facility has been excluded, but supplies no authorized
intrinsic candidate. Raw instruction emission and new assembly wrappers
are outside this task's permitted source forms. The handlers remain guarded.
