body length; the remaining discrepancy is not missing terminal code.

## Page and slot predicates

The original negative-page flag introduces an extra sign-test instruction
around the load-error message block. Computing the predicate directly at
the transition condition as `(next < 0) == false` removes that duplicate.
Converting the selected slot to bool before negating it expresses the
opposite slot and emits retail's sltu/xori/andi sequence. These two changes
must be evaluated together: their respective instruction-count changes
cancel, preserving all subsequent retail block offsets.

The retained version has 24 stack-frame/address differences, three sign-test
register differences at +0x12A8/+0x14B4/+0x14B8, and the commutative transfer
addition operand difference at +0xABC. Retail uses at for the sign test;
native uses v0. All other 34 native functions stay exact and no guard is
removed. Equivalent explicit casts and an assignment inside the predicate
also give 28, without justifying additional source complexity.

Further natural predicate forms, including relational comparisons of the
negative-page bool, do not improve 28. Positive-page flags give 207 or
370 words; cast/de Morgan forms can give 496. Function-scope transfer
values, a local manager pointer, a member reference, and alternate operand
types leave 28..31 words. Moving the memory-base lookup before the transfer
addition does not correct its operand order. The simplest direct predicate
and opposite-slot expression remain the retained draft.

## Inlined message-setter hypothesis

The already documented CDC2Mes message setters are tested through private
copies of their real definitions, without editing their owning unit or
header. This distinguishes storage introduced by an inlined callee from an
unidentified caller local.

| Visible definition in the private caller | Frame bytes | Body bytes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current out-of-line calls | 0x160 | 0x1A64 |
| Inline SetMsgVolumeNo(numbers, count) | 0x1E0 | 0x1AFC |
| Inline SetMsgVolumeNo(numbers, digit_widths, count) | 0x1E0 | 0x1AFC |

| Both SetMsgVolumeNo overloads inline | 0x260 | 0x1B94 |
| Visible non-inline SetMsgVolumeNo(numbers, count) | 0x160 | 0x1A64 |

SetMsgVolumeNoOne obtains retail's 0x1A0 frame, but expands the body and
replaces retail calls with different instructions. The overloads overshoot
the frame. A visible non-inline body leaves all 28 differences intact.
These results do not support a shared-header change or relocating a setter
definition. The 0x40 gap remains without an evidenced local type or extent.
