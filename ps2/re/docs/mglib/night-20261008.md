## Round 2 — mgEndFrame guarded draft 232 → 55

Base `750320ef`, image `chronicletwo_dev:sf-63f7a9e`, production profile.
The guarded draft improves from **232/672 to 55/672** and its body grows from
`0xA34` to retail's declared size `0xA74` (2676). Two source facts:

- **Display-environment writes are whole-register writes.** Retail reloads
  `mgDBuffID` and recomputes `&mgDBuff.disp[mgDBuffID]` after each of the
  BGCOLOR, SMODE2 and DISPFB stores; plain member stores let MWCC keep the
  index and address in registers. Writing those three as
  `*(u_long *) &mgDBuff.disp[mgDBuffID].bgcolor = 0;` (the accepted GS
  whole-register convention, as the DISPLAY store already was) restores
  the reloads (232 → 217 for BGCOLOR/SMODE2 alone). PMODE keeps plain stores:
  its reload already follows the antialiasing branch join.
  In the real SDK these members are `tGS_*` bitfield registers, which is why
  the original code needed the cast; this repository's simplified
  `sceGsDispEnv` declares them `u_long`.
- **DISPFB values are recomputed from the FRAME fields at each use, each
  field widened before shifting**:
  `(u_long) frame->FBP | ((u_long) frame->FBW << 9) | ((u_long) frame->PSM << 15)`
  (the `SCE_GS_SET_DISPFB` expansion). Retail reloads the fields for the
  environment store, `GS DISPFB1` and `GS DISPFB2`, with 64-bit `dsll` and
  no final sign extension; the former `display_base` local computed in
  32 bits, sign-extended, and was reused for DISPFB2. The DISPLAY prefix
  `display_position` stays a local: retail keeps it in `s3` across
  `FlushCache`, `sceGsSwapDBuff` and `sceDmaSync` while reloading the screen
  size for each width/height term.

Declaring `display_position` before `magnification` gives 55; the reverse
gives 58. The position of `frame` among the entry declarations, declaring
`frame`, `magnification` or `display_position` at their assignments, if/else
instead of the ternary frame selection, and `mgDBuffID ?` without `!= 0`
leave 55 or 58. Moving `display_position` after the DISPFB store recomputes
the screen-height term (0xA78, 210); computing it before the frame selection
or splitting the magnification term into a later `|=` gives 58–80.

Remaining 55 words: the documented depth-mask temporaries at
`+0x550..+0x560` (3); `frame` and `display_position` exchange `s1`/`s3`
(retail frame `s1`, magnification `s2`, display position `s3`) with the
resulting schedule of the `+0x744..+0x7C8` window; and the frame-counter
tail at `+0xA0C..+0xA20`, where retail stores the incremented count before
the division-by-zero branch. Activation still needs the local-static
reconciliation recorded above: retail's lazily initialized
`count$580`/`cpu_ratio$583`/`free_ratio$586` with `init$581/584/587` flags
are file-scope `count_580`… definitions while the function is assembly; the
private full-wrapper probe (`.private/nmmenu-r2/mglib/ef-r2-retained-full/`)
reports the resulting extra `.sbss`/`.bss` pieces and unresolved symbols.
