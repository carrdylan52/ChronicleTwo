# CSound initialization near-miss wave, October 8

Base: `3d49d02f5172574aedf56888ba7aa4fb211db3af`. The established MIDI_PORT
layout, field widths and earlier scheduling exclusions in
[notes.md](notes.md) and [midday-20261008.md](midday-20261008.md) are read
before these probes. Fresh m2c output is saved under
`.private/nearmiss-b/m2c/Init__6CSoundFiiii.cpp`. Its flattened guessed
MIDI-state fields are not replacements for the typed 0x124-byte port.

## Result

`Init__6CSoundFiiii` remains **12/480** relocation-masked differing words,
with a `0x77C` body in its `0x780` reservation. Port 11's kind-zero store
still appears at `+0x518` instead of `+0x548`, displacing the four preceding
singleton configuration stores. The sequences realign at `+0x54C`.

The private production wrapper/fixup check confirms one byte problem at
`0x0018A92A` and eight resolved-relocation problems at `+0x518`, `+0x51C`,
`+0x520`, `+0x528`, `+0x52C`, `+0x534`, `+0x538` and `+0x544`.
It checks `0x2DC8` bytes and 700 relocations. These relocation failures are
part of the same misplaced-store interval; a word score alone cannot
authorize promotion. The guard and all source/header/profile files remain
unchanged.

## New exclusions

All probes use MWCC 3.0-011126, canonical flags and the existing Satan's
Fiddle profile in `chronicletwo_dev:sf-d8bf13c`.

- Static definitions of the retail-local midi_state, msinCtx and bgm_info
  retain twelve words. set_spu and TransHdBd are GLOBAL in retail and are
  not localized.
- Chaining upward allocation directions in full or partial groups, at
  either endpoint and in forward/reverse/numeric orders, never improves
  twelve. Combining a zero kind and upward direction for ports 0, 3, 10,
  7, 9, 12 or 11 also fails to improve the schedule. A full direction chain
  combined with moving port 11's zero around the configuration subgroups
  gives 35 words.
- Unsigned port, slot or both counters give 85, 96 and 24 words. Register
  declarations for either or both retain twelve.
- Swapping each adjacent independent special-port assignment pair, while
  excluding the documented direct port-11 movements, retains a twelve-word
  best result. No such pair removes the early zero store.
- A reference to either port-11 field retains twelve. A whole MIDI_STATE
  pointer or reference emits `0x644` bytes and differs in 470 words; this
  different global-address representation is unsuitable for retail.

No field is narrowed, no artificial dependency is introduced and no
floating-argument row is applicable to this integer-only residual.
