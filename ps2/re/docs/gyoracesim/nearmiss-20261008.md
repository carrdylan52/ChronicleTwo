## New exclusions

- CollisionFish is already static. Individually localizing the documented
  local helpers StepGyoRace, StepFish, LaneBattleStep, GetRaceDivision,
  GetCourseR, SetRaceFishParam, CharacterBonus, RndFishParam,
  GetRandomNumber, GetFishData and init_rnd does not change either target.
  Localizing those helpers together also retains 8/360 and 0/204.
- Unsigned order/bucket elements and combinations with the lane counter
  retain eight words. A fresh unsigned inner counter gives 17. Making the
  shared index unsigned with signed count tests gives 246–279 words.
  Halfword or long sorting temporaries give 16 words. These are private
  source-domain trials; the established public types are not narrowed.
- A typed six-element lane record or array alias preserves the eight-word
  result. A reverse chain clearing the six lane counts also retains eight;
  the forward chain gives 14. Capturing both sorting values before their
  stores gives 21; swapping the integer record first gives 263.
- `lane_no <= 5` or its literal-left form gives nine words. A maximum-index
  inner bound gives 19, and an unsigned inner comparison gives nine. The
  unsigned comparison adds `sltu` without fixing the original permutation.
  Combining these forms never improves eight. A not-equal outer endpoint
  gives 38–41 words, depending on its inner bound.
- Scoped optimization level 4 or explicit global optimization on retains
  eight. Level 2 gives 354 words and global optimization off gives 353,
  both with substantially smaller bodies. No pragma is retained.

No new compiler-state row or shared-file proposal follows from these
integer allocation probes. The typed indexed original remains the best
admissible draft.
