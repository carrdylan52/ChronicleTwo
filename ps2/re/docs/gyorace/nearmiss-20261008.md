## Native window data

Two retail-local globals supply the display's texture state:

| Symbol | Retail address | Size | Type and purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| wind_tex | 0x0037E7D8 | 4 | mgCTexture pointer to the window/time-digit atlas |
| WindowTexb | 0x0037E820 | 4 | int texture block containing that atlas |

Both are now unconditional static definitions in gyorace.cpp, replacing
their INCLUDE_BSS markers and the definitions hidden under NONMATCHING.
Their types and purposes are already established by initialization and
display use; `readelf -sW` confirms both retail bindings are LOCAL.
The default whole-unit wrapper/fixup check passes **0x5004 bytes and 979
relocations** with these eight bytes of data supplied naturally.

The change also resolves an actual production-probe compile failure:
enabling only sgSysDrawGyoRace previously left both names undeclared.
Its corrected isolated production probe now compiles and reports just
the known function-byte problem, checking `0x4FF8` bytes and 1,035
relocations. No other data or function problem is introduced. The native
data change does not remove a function guard.

## New source exclusions

- Making the shared fish/minute/lap variable unsigned produces a `0x1264`
  body and 667 words; narrower types also exceed or disrupt the function.
  An unsigned ready-row counter gives 11. Unsigned lap-array selection
  retains ten, while halfword selection variables introduce many differences.
- Giving only the active lap counter a fresh signed or unsigned local,
  preserving the earlier fish and minute variables, gives 34 words.
  The halfword counter forms give 488 or 603 and increase body size.
  This isolates the active scope from the earlier whole-function splits.
- Chaining lap coordinate zeroes retains ten. Chaining both lap indices,
  or all four initializations, gives 11. A five-element row reference
  gives 36; a single row/counter index gives 13.
- Greater-than, negated and unsigned forms of the current-lap comparison
  retain ten. Maximum-index termination gives 11 for the active loop or
  12 for the ready loop.

No counter/row hypothesis improves the retained word score. No float row,
shared-header change or new helper is added.
