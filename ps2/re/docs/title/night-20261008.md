Toy compiles (`.private/nm/ds/t7`–`t10`, neutral translation unit) reproduce
the native allocation exactly: `card1` `s0`, `inport0` `s1`, `inport1` `s2`,
`card0` `s3`, with the second port's `andi` written to `s1`. Integer
snapshots instead follow declaration order. Neither declaration order,
assignment order, a combined declaration, `==`/`else` or `!=` tests, a `bool`
lost flag, explicit `(int)` casts, named call results, a reordered flag
assignment, nor snapshots placed after the `TitleMCCheckNow` return (MWCC does
not hoist them above that branch) gives the second port the lower register.
Changing use counts or range lengths moves both ports (`s2`/`s0` and
similar), so the choice follows the ports' uses and live ranges rather than
their declaration or numbering. Retail's `inport1` `s1` / `inport0` `s2`
(both `andi` in place) therefore needs a different use structure that has not
been identified.

No form exchanges the two port registers without disturbing the rest of the
block. `TitleBootInit` was not attempted; it is the placement-new blocker
