## CommonBoardDraw

The inherited guarded draft remains at 29/888 differing words. Its
0xDD8-byte body equals the unpadded retail length in .mwcats at section
offset 0x6B9C (`02 00 d8 0d 10 4f 22 00`, address 0x00224F10).
Direct material-line array indexing without the inherited BoardLine helper
retains the established 43-word result.

The first 16 differences exchange s1 and s4. Retail assigns s1 to the
left pixel coordinate after the conversion at +0x22C. At +0x2A8, s4
temporarily addresses the first UV segment's signed top byte; at +0x308
the same register is reused for the row's bottom pixel coordinate. Native
exchanges those registers. This is a UV-component pointer, not a colour
pointer. The remaining 13 differences exchange the material-line pointer
(retail s1 at +0x5CC) and integer top coordinate (retail s3 at +0x638).
The board UV rectangle pointer uses s2 in both inherited drafts.

New typed aliases test whether the UV row/segment lifetime accounts for
this allocation. They use the documented signed s8 arrays and constant
component indices, without raw pointer induction or new helpers.
